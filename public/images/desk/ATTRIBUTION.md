## Apple product imagery

- `reference/ipad-pro-space-black.png`
  - Derived from Apple's 13-inch iPad Pro product image (Space Black)
  - Processing: white background removed and transparent bounds cropped
  - Source: https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-ipad/ipad-pro

- `reference/ipad-pro-editorial.png`
  - Derived from user-provided iPad photograph
  - Processing: perspective mask isolates the iPad while preserving the screen content

- `reference/ipad-pro-dark-clock.webp`
  - Derived from user-provided iPad photograph
  - Processing: macOS Vision foreground segmentation, edge contraction, and WebP optimization

- `reference/apple-pencil-pro.png`
  - Apple Pencil Pro product image
  - Source: https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mx2d3am/a/apple-pencil-pro
